# NVM AquaFeed Platform

## Cage Management

Document ID : AQ-CAG-020

Version : 1.0

--------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose
--------------------------------------------------

Every cage is an independent production unit.

A cage may change feeding line.

A cage may change selector eye.

History shall always be preserved.
